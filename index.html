About Us

Learn more about our mission and values

Our Story

Founded in 2018, SEO Academy was born from a simple observation: most SEO courses were too technical and intimidating for beginners. Our founder, a marketing professional with no coding background, struggled to find quality resources that explained SEO in plain English. This frustration sparked an idea to create a course specifically designed for non-technical learners. Today, we've helped over 50,000 students worldwide master SEO fundamentals and improve their online visibility.

What started as a passion project has evolved into a comprehensive learning platform trusted by entrepreneurs, small business owners, and marketing professionals globally. Our commitment to making SEO accessible has never wavered, and we continue to innovate and improve our curriculum based on student feedback and industry changes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize SEO education and empower individuals to take control of their online presence. We believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality SEO knowledge, regardless of their technical background or budget constraints. By breaking down complex concepts into simple, actionable steps, we enable our students to make informed decisions about their digital marketing strategies.

We're committed to maintaining the highest standards of education through continuous learning, regular curriculum updates, and exceptional customer support. Our core values—accessibility, integrity, and excellence—guide every decision we make. We measure our success not just by the number of students we reach, but by the tangible results they achieve in their careers and businesses.
