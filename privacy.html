Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, track your activity, and improve your browsing experience. We use both session-based and persistent cookies on our platform.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic platform functionality and security
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your activity across websites for targeted advertising
  • Analytics Cookies: Collect data about how you use our services

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we use other tracking technologies such as:

  • Web beacons and pixels
  • Local storage and session storage
  • Mobile device identifiers
  • Log files and server data

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to accept or reject cookies, or be notified when a cookie is sent. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our platform. You can also opt out of targeted advertising through industry-standard opt-out mechanisms and your device settings.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security infrastructure includes:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit

  • Employee training on data protection practices
  • Incident response procedures

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use our platform at your own risk. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.
